# Data notice and source terms

The aggregate permit outputs are derivative analyses of the City of San Diego's **Approvals for development projects** dataset:

- Dataset: https://data.sandiego.gov/datasets/development-permits/
- Terms of Use: https://data.sandiego.gov/help/guides/terms/

The City portal describes its open data as available for use and redistribution, subject to its Terms of Use. The City provides the source “as is,” does not warrant its accuracy, and does not endorse this analysis. Users of this repository and its generated outputs should review the current City terms directly.

The San Diego cost ranges are sourced from the publisher-maintained Renology Renovation Cost Index:

- Cost Index: https://www.therenology.com/cost-index
- Methodology: https://www.therenology.com/methodology
- Machine-readable source: https://www.therenology.com/cost-index/data.json

They are total-project planning ranges, not permit valuations, transaction prices, bids, quotes, appraisals, or guarantees.

Only aggregate outputs are published. Source fields containing addresses, coordinates, permit holders, detailed scope text, or other project-level information are deliberately excluded.
