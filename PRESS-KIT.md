# Press and reuse kit

## One-sentence description

The San Diego Renovation Permit & Cost Pulse is a reproducible, privacy-preserving analysis of 2026 City of San Diego issued renovation-related projects, paired separately with Renology total-project planning cost ranges.

## Defensible findings in the July 19, 2026 snapshot

- **1,601 unique relevant City projects** met the documented residential and keyword filters through the latest included issue date, July 16, 2026.
- Those projects produced **2,719 project-category observations** because a project can name more than one scope.
- The snapshot includes **741 ADU**, **385 kitchen**, **658 bathroom**, **60 roofing**, and **875 general-remodel** project-category observations.
- The median elapsed interval among relevant unique projects was **150 calendar days** from City project creation to the earliest matching issued approval.

Do not describe the elapsed interval as City staff processing time, construction duration, or an expected timeline for a new application. Do not add category counts together and call the result unique projects.

## Suggested story angles

1. **What San Diego’s 2026 permit records reveal about the renovation scopes residents are pursuing**
2. **ADUs versus kitchens and baths: a public-data look at San Diego project activity**
3. **A privacy-preserving way to turn municipal permit records into a homeowner planning brief**
4. **How an AI-readable civic-data release separates permit activity from commercial cost benchmarks**
